 CALCULATOR ALGORITHIM

 STEP 1 - Start the calculator.

 STEP 2 - The user inputs the first number into the calculator.

 STEP 3 - The user selects the operator they want to use.

 STEP 4 - The user inputs the second number into the calculator.

 STEP 5 - The user then selects the equals to (=) operator.

 STEP 6 - The calculator displays the final result.


 SORTING NUMBERS 

 (75, 7, 3, 89, 0.49, 13)

 STEP 1 - Take into account the numbers oresented to you.

 STEP 2 - Compare all the numbers.

 STEP 3 - Start with the smallest number and write it down.

 STEP 4 - Compare all the numbers to the smallest number.

 STEP 5 - Whatever number is larger than the first one but smaller than the others, should be written down as the second number 
 in the sequence.
 
 STEP 6 - Continue the doing step 5 until all the numbers in the sequence have been accounted for.

 STEP 7 - Write down the new order of numbers.
